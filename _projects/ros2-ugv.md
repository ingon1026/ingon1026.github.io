## Scope

As part of a laboratory multi-UXV team project, I was responsible for setting up an unmanned ground vehicle and building its autonomous-driving pipeline.

## Contribution

- Set up the UGV platform and ROS environment.
- Integrated CAN communication, the robot SDK, an Ouster LiDAR, an Intel RealSense D435i camera, and IMU sensing.
- Configured SLAM and navigation workflows.
- Produced setup and integration guides to make the platform easier to reproduce and maintain.
